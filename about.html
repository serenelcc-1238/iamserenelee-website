Serene made the move into real estate after a career spanning procurement, customer service, and data analysis — and it shows in how she works. Before recommending a single listing, she starts with the numbers: your income, your CPF, your existing HDB, and what a genuinely comfortable move looks like for your household, not just what a bank might approve.

Her focus spans two related transitions: HDB owners who've completed their Minimum Occupation Period and are weighing whether to move into private property, and buyers exploring new launch condominiums who want a clear-eyed read on financing, timing, and value — not just a showflat tour.
